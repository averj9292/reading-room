Teach, check, and revisit

Original teacher-led practice for five short vowels and the sh/ch teams. Use after reviewing the learner’s lesson and support counts. These checks supplement the app; they do not assign a reading level or establish mastery.

Before you start: confirm the learner knows the consonant sounds and any other patterns in your selected words. These are unfamiliar to that lesson’s app word bank, but may be familiar to the child. Print from your browser. Keep any student names and written notes offline.

Choose the next step: if reading is independent, try another sentence or follow your teaching sequence. If support is needed, model the specific difficulty, practise together, then check a different example. Adjust the app plan when appropriate. Three words are a quick observation, not a validated pass threshold.

Short a

Teach together: Keep the middle vowel short. Say each sound, then blend. Model “cat”, then let the learner blend it. Use a different familiar example together before the check.

Fresh-word check

cab ham lap

Show one word at a time without saying it first. Listen to the complete response. If help is needed, teach it, then keep that response separate from independent reading.

Read a sentence

A cat sat. Dad had a ham.

This sentence revisits familiar words. Check any additional patterns and vocabulary before using it; it is not a controlled decodable passage.

Later review

dad van wax

On another day, check these words without a model first. Mix in two previously taught words. If difficulty returns, revisit the teaching rather than counting the earlier completion as lasting learning.
